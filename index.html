    Projects

    Spear of Ares: Tube-Deployed Reentry Vehicle for a Mars Weather Station

    Undergraduate Capstone, Aug 2022 – May 2023 · University of Idaho · Partner: NASA Ames Research Center

    With a seven-person interdisciplinary team, I helped design, build, and flight-test a tube-deployed reentry vehicle prototype for a potential Mars weather station. The payload combined a spring-deployed drag skirt, custom multilayer PCBs, and an ESP32 flight computer running FreeRTOS with publish–subscribe inter-process communication, supported by a ground tracking GUI. We validated it with a high-altitude balloon drop in February 2023, and presented the work at the University of Idaho Engineering Design EXPO 2023.
